The Wheel of Change

Standard 2-hour turn protocols treat all patients equally and lack structural variety. Our platform utilizes an 8-stage Wheel of Change, cycling patients through precise, medically validated postures to entirely alleviate localized pressure.

Dynamic Tracking

Biological Sensor Mapping

Module 03

The Escalation Journey

Alarm fatigue is a critical ECRI health hazard. Code Blue uses a chronological, 3-tier escalation protocol. It only broadcasts to higher levels if bedside interventions fail to occur, protecting nursing cognitive load.
